XDesk

Open your Xbox game library from a console-style dashboard, play, and jump back to your collection whenever you want.

Download for Windows ↓Release & source

Version 1.0.9 · Portable ZIP · 62.8 MiB · Includes source and licenses

01 / LIBRARY

Your games in one place

Import Xbox ISO and XISO files or scan a whole folder.

02 / ARTWORK

Automatic cover art

XDesk downloads matching covers. Choose Background image on the home screen to set your own PNG, JPEG, or BMP.

03 / PLAY

Dashboard on demand

Press F1 to reopen the dashboard during play. The on-screen Dashboard button hides while a game runs.

04 / CONTROLLERS

DualSense and trigger response

Set DualSense L2 and R2 effects independently. Switch gameplay effects and diagnostic tests on or off from the redesigned Settings menu.

Get started

  1. Extract the Windows ZIP and open XDesk.exe.
  2. Use System settings to select your Xbox firmware and virtual hard disk.
  3. Open Games to add an ISO, XISO, or game folder.

Firmware, game images, and virtual disks are supplied separately. XDesk has no built-in app updater.
